Summarise a list

average = sum(b["pages"] for b in books) / len(books)
shortest = min(books, key=lambda b: b["pages"])
longest = max(books, key=lambda b: b["pages"])
ranking = sorted(books, key=lambda b: b["pages"], reverse=True)
long_ones = [b for b in books if b["pages"] > 300]

key says what to compare. lambda b: … is a small function without a name.
