## Objectives

1. argue a position in English or French with sources
2. answer an opposing argument
3. distinguish evidence from opinion
4. think about your own use of AI tools

## Sources to prepare (read both sides)

Check the date and the limits of each source.

**Motion A: evidence for** — [Article on developer jobs, CNBC (Oct 2026)](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/11/ai-software-developer-jobs.html): young developers' employment has fallen.

**Motion A: evidence against** — [US Bureau of Labor Statistics projections 2025–2035](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/ecopro_08272026.htm) and the [Occupational Outlook for software developers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/Computer-and-Information-Technology/software-developers.htm); [METR study (July 2025)](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/): experienced developers were slower with AI. METR [later said this result is outdated](https://x.com/METR_Evals/status/2026355544668385373).

**Motion B** — [European Parliament report on copyright and generative AI](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/doceo/document/A-10-2026-0019_EN.html); [ODECO report on openness and AI training](https://data.europa.eu/en/news-events/news/navigating-legal-challenges-ai-training-new-odeco-report-explores-data-openness); against any new condition: the principle of open reuse in the [Eurostat notice](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/help/copyright-notice).
