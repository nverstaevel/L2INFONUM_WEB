## Step 3 · Rehearse (20 min)

The demonstration is **5 minutes, in English**. Prepare one or two sentences for each line:

| Minutes | Say and show |
|---|---|
| 1 | *Our question is… The data comes from… under the licence…* |
| 3 | the application live: *When I choose…, the server…* |
| 1 | *With more time, we would…* |

- [ ] Rehearse once with a timer.
- [ ] Each member can explain every line of code.

**Milestone.** Under 5 minutes.

## Step 4 · Submit and demonstrate (70 min)

- [ ] Make a ZIP of the project **without** `.venv`.
- [ ] Upload it, before your demonstration, to the platform given in class.
- [ ] Present.

**Milestone.** Submitted and presented.
