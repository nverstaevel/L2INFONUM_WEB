title: Choosing and reading a chart
---
A line shows a change over time, bars compare values, a pie shows the parts of one whole. Check: where the axis starts, missing years, unit, source. Bars should start at zero.

## Example

```text
Question                      Chart
How did it change?            line
Which one is higher?          bar
What share of the total?      pie (few parts)
```

## Common mistakes

- A truncated axis that makes a small change look huge.
