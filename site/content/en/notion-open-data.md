title: Open data and licences
---
**Open data** can be used, changed and shared by anyone. A **licence** says under which conditions. Most often you must name the source (**attribution**). Before using a dataset, find: the producer, the licence, the date, the unit.

## Example

```text
Source: Eurostat, dataset tin00028, downloaded on 8 January 2027.
Changes: kept one indicator and countries only.
```

## Common mistakes

- A page that is public is not necessarily open: check the licence.
- Not saying that you filtered or changed the data.
